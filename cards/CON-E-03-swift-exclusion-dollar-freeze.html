CONFLICT Economic (E)
CON-E-03
SWIFT Exclusion and Dollar Asset Freeze
Escalation Risk
CRITICAL
⚫
Actors: United States
Action
The US Treasury Department removes all major Chinese state-owned banks from the SWIFT financial messaging system and freezes all US-held Chinese Treasury bonds. This is the Nuclear Option of financial statecraft.
Immediate Effect
China loses 50% of its Foreign Reserves. International trade in US Dollars becomes impossible for Chinese firms.
Potential Outcomes
Best Case
The Chinese economy crashes, leading to widespread domestic protests and forcing a cessation of military operations.
Partial Success
China successfully pivots to its Digital Yuan and CIPS system for trade with Russia/ASEAN, but its global influence is halved.
Backfire
China responds by seizing all US corporate assets (Apple, Tesla, etc.) on the mainland, leading to a US stock market Meltdown.
Counter-Cards
China Digital Yuan Expansion (E)
Constraints & Limitations
  • SWIFT exclusion requires SWIFT's Belgian board consensus and EU agreement — not a unilateral US decision
  • China has been building CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) specifically to survive SWIFT exclusion
  • The weaponization of the dollar accelerates global de-dollarization efforts — potential long-term damage to US financial power
  • China's $1.5T in US Treasury holdings creates a mutually assured financial destruction dynamic
Key Assumptions
  • EU agrees to join the SWIFT exclusion rather than pursuing continued economic engagement
  • China's CIPS system isn't yet capable of substituting for SWIFT at the volume required for wartime supply chains
  • The freeze doesn't trigger China's retaliatory dumping of US Treasury holdings
Strategic Notes
▼
The March 2022 freezing of Russia's $300B and SWIFT exclusion was the most significant use of financial power since WWII. China holds approximately $3T in US Treasuries by 2030 — about 25% of global GDP. A SWIFT exclusion of China would cause a global depression. China has built CIPS specifically as a SWIFT alternative — by 2030, CIPS handles roughly 15% of SWIFT's volume. US corporations in China have hundreds of billions in assets at risk.
Historical Analogues
▼
  • **Iran SWIFT Exclusion (March 2012):** Swift disconnected Iranian banks following US/EU sanctions — the first use of SWIFT as a sanctions weapon, cutting Iran's oil revenue by 50%
  • **Russia SWIFT Partial Exclusion (February 2022):** Seven Russian banks were disconnected following the Ukraine invasion — but not all banks, specifically to allow energy payments to continue, demonstrating the limits of financial weaponization
  • **Bretton Woods and the Dollar (1944-1971):** The dollar's reserve currency status was built over decades — Nixon's 1971 gold window closure showed that financial architecture can be changed unilaterally, but at significant cost
