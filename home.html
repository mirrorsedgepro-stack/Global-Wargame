About the Project

Design

Players represent strategic actors pressing objectives across four DIME domains — Diplomatic, Informational, Military, Economic — through three phases: Competition, Conflict, and Crisis.

Each card carries a risk score (1–10), primary actors and a keyword-flavoured DIME vector. The adjudicator computes net effect from attacker posture vs defender posture, applies a phase multiplier, and resolves to BEST, PARTIAL or BACKFIRE. The world state updates — country DIME baselines, GDP, stockpiles, morale — and the global commodity market nudges in response.
